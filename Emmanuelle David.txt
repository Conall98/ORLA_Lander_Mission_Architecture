My idea is to find super cheap ways of transporting things in space that people have not thought of before. For me, cost is the great inhibitor of space exploration. if we can reduce the cost and make space more accessible for more potential innovators, then we will start to move towards the space faring future (manned lunar bases, in space manufacturing, manned missions to mars, an economy of people living and working off of the planet Earth) which has been envisaged for so long.

My ideas about doing that are designing low cost mission architectures, building tools to calculate the lifecycle cost of many different archiectures simultaneously, and designing low-cost spacecraft to carry out those missions.
